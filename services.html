01

Taxation & tax advisory

For anyone with income tax to work out, file or answer questions about: salaried employees with more than one income, professionals, firms and companies.

What the work involves

  • Income tax returns and the computation behind each one
  • Advance tax estimates during the year
  • Replies to notices, and representation in assessments
  • Planning the tax on salary, property, capital gains and business income

02

GST & compliance

For businesses registered under GST, and for those working out whether they need to be.

What the work involves

  • Registration, amendments and cancellation
  • Monthly or quarterly returns
  • Reconciling input tax credit with supplier filings
  • Annual returns, and replies to department queries

What to bring tap to tick

03

Audit & assurance

For companies and LLPs that need an audit by law, businesses that cross the tax-audit threshold, and owners who want an independent check.

What the work involves

  • Statutory audits of companies and LLPs
  • Tax audits, where turnover or receipts cross the limit
  • Internal audits and reviews of specific processes
  • Certificates that banks and regulators ask for

What to bring tap to tick

04

Accounting & bookkeeping

For businesses that want their books kept, or checked, by someone outside the business.

What the work involves

  • Day-to-day bookkeeping
  • Bank, party and ledger reconciliations
  • Month-end and year-end closing
  • Fixed asset and other registers

What to bring tap to tick

05

Financial reporting

For anyone who has to show their numbers to someone else: a bank, a regulator, a partner or an investor.

What the work involves

  • Annual financial statements with notes and schedules
  • Projections and CMA data for bank loans
  • Periodic reports for owners and management

What to bring tap to tick

06

Business & financial advisory

For decisions with a tax or accounting side, made before the paperwork rather than after it.

What the work involves

  • Choosing a structure: proprietorship, partnership, LLP or company
  • Registrations when a business starts or changes
  • The tax effect of a sale, purchase or restructuring
