The year at a glance

April to March,mapped.

Most tax and accounting work follows the Indian financial year. Hover or tap a row to find it on the dial.

  • GST returnsEvery month, or every quarter, depending on how the business is registered.
  • Advance taxPaid in four instalments during the year it relates to.
  • Closing the booksThe year ends on 31 March. Accounts are closed and reconciled in the weeks after.
  • AuditsWhere an audit applies, it usually runs through the months after year-end.
  • Income tax returnsDue after year-end. Cases that need an audit have a later date.

A general pattern only. Due dates are set by law, change by notification and differ from case to case.
